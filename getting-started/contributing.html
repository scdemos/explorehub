Contribution philosophy

The playbook is narrow, opinionated, and pinned. A chapter belongs here when the pattern is already shipped — ideally inspectable in scdemos/demo — and the write-up stays a short reference, not a tutorial.

Prefer extending an existing chapter over adding a near-duplicate. Quality over volume.

Anti-goals

  • Not a boilerplate, starter kit, or framework
  • Not Adobe-official architecture
  • Not a mirror of aem.live or docs.da.live — link out instead
  • Not patterns without shippable reference code
  • Not long tutorials, open-ended governance, or unpinned main branch links
