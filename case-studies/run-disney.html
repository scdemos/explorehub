Overview

runDisney promotes marathons and experiences tied to Disney parks. The site mixes inspirational storytelling, event detail pages, and registration flows that spike traffic around race calendars. EDS lets marketing rotate campaigns while forms and integrations stay maintainable (sample).

Reference for event-led consumer brands: seasonal heroes, countdown modules, and worker-backed forms.

Business challenge

Teams launch races on predictable calendars but need agility for creative campaigns, pricing changes, and sold-out states. Registration must feel on-brand while posting data securely to downstream systems.

  • Swap homepage and event heroes without redeploying code.
  • Capture registrations with validation and bot protection (sample).
  • Personalize promos by audience or geography (sample).

Solution on EDS

Marketing pages are block-composed in DA; forms post to edge workers that hold secrets and integrate with CRM or event platforms. Experimentation may gate hero modules or CTAs during enrollment windows (sample).

  • Event detail templates with schedule, FAQ, and CTA blocks.
  • JSON-defined forms rendered in blocks, processed by workers.
  • Campaign sections with metadata-driven backgrounds and typography.
