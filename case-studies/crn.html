Overview

CRN operates a high-velocity editorial property where homepage composition, article templates, and search are first-class concerns. EDS supports rapid publishing, taxonomy-driven rails, and shared theming across related brands in the portfolio (sample).

Reference when scoping B2B media: homepage factories, author tooling, faceted search, and RSS/syndication.

Business challenge

Editorial teams publish continuously; homepage and category pages must refresh without developer involvement. Search must stay relevant across years of archives while sponsorship zones remain manageable.

  • Composable homepage templates per brand theme.
  • Fast article detail pages with related content and SEO metadata.
  • Search and faceting that scale to large indexes (sample).
  • Feeds for partners and syndication (sample).

Solution on EDS

Articles and hubs are authored as structured content, published through EDS, and surfaced via blocks for rails, top stories, and related modules. Query-index powers on-site search; workers may expose RSS/JSON feeds for syndication.

  • Homepage builder patterns with reusable “top stories” and category rails.
  • Article template with author bylines, taxonomy, and related articles block.
  • Search results template with filters tied to index metadata (sample).
