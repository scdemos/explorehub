Overview

Revolt delivers a brand-heavy, content-rich experience where editorial teams rotate heroes, promos, and show rails frequently. EDS gives authors block-level control while keeping front-end performance acceptable on media-heavy pages.

Reference this case study for marketing-led media sites: video embeds, carousels, featured stories, and campaign landing patterns.

Business challenge

Editorial and marketing needed to launch shows, tours, and cultural moments quickly without developer bottlenecks — while preserving a distinctive visual brand and acceptable LCP on image- and video-led pages.

  • Reuse hero and card patterns across hubs without duplicate code paths.
  • Support embedded video and third-party players without blocking first paint (sample).
  • Enable experimentation on promos and featured rails (sample).

Solution on EDS

Authors compose pages from blocks (hero, cards, carousels, embeds) with section metadata for spacing and background treatments. Core scripts manage phased loading so media does not dominate the critical path.

  • Template-like hub pages built from repeatable block sequences.
  • Lazy media and embed initialization in delayed phase (sample).
  • Optional personalization / A/B hooks for promotional modules (sample).
