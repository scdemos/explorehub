Travel & consumer retail

High-traffic consumer marketing on Edge Delivery — weekly deal swaps, dual product lines, and clear paths into booking engines the stack does not own.

Travel Consumer Migration
Anonymous case study. A composite migration narrative for this industry — useful for planning your own EDS program, not a named customer endorsement.

Overview

A travel group operated separate consumer brands for flights and package holidays. Marketing rotated deals weekly; destination guides needed rich imagery; conversion happened in booking apps outside the CMS. Legacy templates slowed merchandising; mobile performance suffered on promo-heavy pages.

EDS unified the merchandising layer: authors maintain deal grids and hero offers as blocks, indexes surface offers and destinations, fragments link the brands — while booking stays in specialized systems.

Where the program started

  • Deal pages rebuilt from templates each sale window — engineering bottleneck.
  • Two brands with overlapping components but separate codebases.
  • Offer data sometimes authored manually, sometimes fed from external systems — inconsistent.
  • Heavy imagery and countdown modules on mobile without a performance budget.

Migration journey

  1. Unify blocks across brands. Shared cards, product grids, and heroes; brand differentiation via fragments and tokens.
  2. Model offers as authored tables + index. Metadata for route, price band, and season feeds query-index for search and grids.
  3. Wire CTAs to booking. Every promo block ends in a deep link pattern authors understand — no fake “checkout” on EDS.
  4. Experiment on heroes. Sale windows use experimentation on hero CTAs and featured deals.
  5. Migrate destination content first. Evergreen guides moved to EDS; volatile deal pages followed once grids were stable.
  6. Align analytics. Consistent campaign parameters on outbound booking links for measurement across properties.

What landed on EDS

  • Weekly deal and destination updates without template redeploys.
  • Shared block library across flights and holidays brands.
  • Search and filter UI over offer metadata.
  • Mobile-friendly promo pages with disciplined image loading.

Target architecture

flowchart LR
  Marketing[Marketing authors] --> DA[Document Authoring]
  DA --> EDS[EDS marketing pages]
  EDS --> Index[Offer / destination index]
  Index --> UI[Search / grid blocks]
  EDS --> Booking[External booking apps]

EDS owns storytelling and merchandising; inventory and payment stay in booking systems of record.

Lessons from the journey

  • Do not replicate booking on EDS. Marketing’s job is to qualify and hand off — not to own carts.
  • Indexes need a source of truth. Decide what is authored vs fed — hybrid models need clear rules.
  • Two brands, one library. Fragment and token differences beat forked codebases.
  • Seasonality is the roadmap. Migrate before peak sale windows, not during them.
