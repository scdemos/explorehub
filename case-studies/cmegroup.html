Overview

CME Group operates a large, compliance-sensitive marketing and education surface on EDS. The program prioritizes predictable performance, clear ownership of plugins in Document Authoring, and patterns that scale across product lines without forking the core delivery stack.

Use this case study when scoping regulated-industry sites: deep navigation, external link policy, search over structured content, and distributed authoring with guardrails.

Business challenge

The team needed a modern authoring experience without sacrificing control over what ships to production — especially for links, disclosures, and performance on market-moving news cycles.

  • Reduce time-to-publish for regulated copy while keeping review workflows intact.
  • Unify plugin and library configuration so regional teams do not drift.
  • Improve discoverability across products, education, and event content.
  • Maintain Core Web Vitals under traffic spikes (sample goal).

Solution on EDS

Marketing and product content is authored in DA, delivered through EDS, and extended with project-specific blocks and scripts. Sidekick plugins are managed centrally; authors work against shared libraries rather than one-off page builds.

  • Composable sections and blocks for product storytelling and market education.
  • Search and listing experiences backed by query-index patterns (see related playbook).
  • Domain-aware link handling in core scripts for internal vs external destinations (sample).
  • Performance budgeting in eager / lazy / delayed load phases (sample).

Architecture

flowchart LR
  Authors[Marketing authors] --> DA[Document Authoring]
  DA --> Git[Content bus / Git]
  Git --> EDS[EDS pipeline]
  EDS --> CDN[CDN]
  CDN --> Users[Visitors]
  EDS --> Index[query-index.json]

Authors never touch production HTML directly. Search and listing UIs read structured indexes generated at publish time; the browser loads decorated blocks from EDS (sample topology).
