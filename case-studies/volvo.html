Overview

Volvo Cars represents a large-scale consumer brand on EDS: rich imagery, vehicle storytelling, campaign landings, and localized markets. The implementation emphasizes design consistency, performance on image-heavy templates, and author-friendly block variants.

Use as a reference when explaining EDS to automotive or lifestyle brands with multi-market rollouts.

Business challenge

Markets needed autonomy within a strict design system — new campaigns and vehicle launches every quarter without breaking global performance or accessibility standards.

  • Balance global design system with local offers and copy.
  • Keep LCP strong on full-bleed imagery and video loops (sample).
  • Integrate third-party configurators without owning checkout in EDS (sample).

Solution on EDS

Pages are assembled from a broad block palette (hero, tabs, cards, fragments) with section metadata controlling layout width and visual theme. JSON-driven or templated pages may back listing experiences where appropriate.

  • Reusable campaign templates with block variants per vehicle line.
  • Fragment-based shared chrome (header/footer) across markets (sample).
  • Structured data feeds for news / press or vehicle metadata (sample).
