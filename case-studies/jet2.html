Overview

Jet2 operates high-traffic consumer surfaces for flights and package holidays. EDS supports promotional grids, destination storytelling, and hand-off points to booking engines while keeping marketing pages fast during peak seasons (sample).

Reference for travel retail: deal tiles, destination hubs, search-led merchandising, and cross-property navigation.

Business challenge

Marketing must rotate deals and destinations quickly while conversion paths stay measurable. Pages should remain performant when loaded with imagery, price callouts, and promotional timers (sample).

  • Merchandise offers without rebuilding templates each week.
  • Connect EDS content to booking flows owned outside Helix (sample).
  • Target promotions by audience or route (sample).

Solution on EDS

Authors maintain deal grids, hero promos, and destination guides as blocks. Search or index-driven modules surface offers; experimentation can tune hero CTAs. Booking completion happens in specialized apps linked from EDS pages (sample).

  • Card and carousel blocks for deals and destinations.
  • Search / filter UI over offer metadata (sample).
  • Clear CTAs into flights vs holidays funnels.
